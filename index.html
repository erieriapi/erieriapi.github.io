Usage Limits

API access is governed by subscription-based usage tiers. Usage is calculated as the product of unique jobs and unique job locations:

Each job and area successfully accessed may be reused in future requests without increasing usage. Please reach out to your ERI account manager to understand your subscription-based usage limits.

Important Notes

  • Usage is tracked across all API keys associated with the same account.
  • Only jobs and areas available under the customer's subscription may be accessed.
  • Usage allocations are not automatically reset.
  • Invalid or unmatched jobs and job locations will return appropriate error messages.
  • Once the usage limit is reached, additional API access may be restricted until the account usage profile is reset.
