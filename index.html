Usage Limits

API access is governed by subscription-based usage tiers. Usage is calculated as:

Unique Job IDs × Unique Area IDs

Each job and area successfully accessed is stored and may be reused in future requests without increasing usage. New Job IDs and Area IDs are added to a user's usage profile only after salary data has been successfully returned.

Available Tiers

  • 2.5K - Up to 2,500 Job × Area combinations
  • 25K - Up to 25,000 Job × Area combinations
  • 250K - Up to 250,000 Job × Area combinations

Important Notes

  • Usage is tracked across all API keys associated with the same account.
  • Only jobs and areas available under the customer's subscription may be accessed.
  • Usage allocations are not automatically reset.
  • Invalid or unmatched Job IDs and Area IDs will return appropriate error messages.
  • Once the usage limit is reached, additional Job IDs or Area IDs may be restricted until the account usage profile is reset.
